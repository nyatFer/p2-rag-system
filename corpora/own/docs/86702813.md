# 可い (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

可い (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Regular shift from Classical Japanese ク活用 (ku-katsuyō) adjective 可し (beshi) to modern -i adjective.

Adjective

可い • (bei) -i (adverbial 可く (beku))

(dialectal Kanto, Tōhoku, Hokkaidō, archaic) should; must

Usage notes

As an adjective, it can be considered to only be used in place of Standard Japanese 終止形 (shūshikei, “terminal form”) べし (beshi) and 連体形 (rentaikei, “attributive form”) べき (beki), as all other inflected forms are identical.

Particle

可い • (bei)

(dialectal Kanto, Tōhoku, Hokkaidō) sentence-ending particle indicating volition or supposition
