Etymology

(ratio, compare, equal, match): From Middle Chinese 比 (MC pjijX).

(eighth hexagram of the I Ching): From Middle Chinese 比 (MC pjijH) or 比 (MC bjijH).

(Philippines): Short form of the ateji (当て字) spelling 比律賓 (Firipin).
