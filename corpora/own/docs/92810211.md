# でも (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

でも (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

で (de, conjunctive ("て-form") of copula だ/です) +‎ も (mo, “even; too”).

When でも follows a noun, で is the conjunctive or "て-form" of the copula だ/です. で can also appear in the conjunctive form of certain classes of verbs; in that case, でも can be analyzed as the conjunctive form of the verb + the inclusive particle も, and means "even if"; compare with the allomorph ても used for different classes of verbs.

Particle

でも • (de mo)

even

先生でも間違えるよ。

Sensei demo machigaeru yo.

Even teachers make mistakes.

or

お茶でも、コーヒーでも、どうですか？

O-cha de mo, kōhī de mo, dō desu ka?

How about some tea or coffee?

or something

酒でも飲もうよ。

Sake de mo nomō yo.

Let's have a drink or something.

気づかないとでも思った？

Kizukanai to de mo omotta?

Did you think I wouldn't notice or something?

気づかないとでも？

Kizukanai to de mo?

[Did you think] I wouldn't notice or something?

Conjunction

でも • (de mo)

(informal) but, however

でも、私はそう思わない。

De mo, watashi wa sō omowanai.

But I don't think so.

Etymology 2

で (de, “at; in”) +‎ も (mo, “even; too”)

also in

日本でも英語を勉強する。

Nihon de mo eigo o benkyō suru.

In Japan also, we study English.

宇宙からでも万里の長城が見える。

Uchū kara de mo Banri no Chōjō ga mieru.

Even from space you can see the Great Wall of China.

Etymology 3

でも • (de mo)

(after interrogatives) any, without restriction

食事制限はないので何でも大丈夫です。

Shokujiseigen wa nainode Nan de mo Daijōbudesu .

I don't have any dietary restrictions so anything is fine.

この仕事は誰でもいい

kono Shigoto wa Dare demo ī

Anybody will do for this work

(compare with か and も, which express the indefinite pronouns "some" (something, someone) and "all"/"none", respectively, when used after interrogatives)
