# 暮らす (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

暮らす (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Verb

暮らす • (kurasu) transitive or intransitive godan (stem 暮らし (kurashi), past 暮らした (kurashita))

(transitive) to spend (the daytime of one day)

Antonym: 明かす (akasu)

一日を読書で暮らす。

Ichinichi o dokusho de kurasu.

He spends a whole day reading.

(transitive) to spend (a long time, longer than one day)

余生は郷里で暮らしたい。

Yosei wa kyōri de kurashitai.

I want to spend the rest of my life in my hometown.

(intransitive) to live, to lead a life, to get on

少ない収入でなんとか暮らしている。

Sukunai shūnyū de nantoka kurashite iru.

I managed to live on with a low income.

(auxiliary, with a verb in ren'yōkei) to spend a whole day doing

泣き暮らす ― naki kurasu ― to spend a whole day weeping
