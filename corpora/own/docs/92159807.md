# また (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

また (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

From Old Japanese, ultimately from Proto-Japonic *mata. First cited to the Nihon Shoki of 720 CE.

Adverb

また • (mata)

又, 復: again; as well

またやったか？

Mata yatta ka?

They did it again?

また連絡します。

Mata renraku shimasu.

I will contact you again.

亦: as well; likewise

Conjunction

また • (mata)

and, also

Noun

また • (mata)

又: another (one); also; again

またの名を

mata no na o

also known as...

またを約束する

mata o yakusoku suru

to promise to meet again

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana, as また (mata).
