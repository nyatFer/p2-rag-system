# っぽい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

っぽい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Emphasis of earlier form ぽい (poi), in turn apparently an Edo-period shift from 多い (ōi, “many, much, lots of”), previously read and written as おほい (ohoi ← ofoi ← opoi).

Suffix

っぽい • (-ppoi) -i (adverbial っぽく (-ppoku))

-like, -ish, -esque

子供っぽい

kodomoppoi

childish

彼女の目は緑っぽい色です。

Kanojo no me wa midorippoi iro desu.

Her eyes are of a greenish color.

レトロっぽい

retoroppoi

retroesque

フュージョンっぽい

fyūjonppoi

fusionesque

Usage notes

Follows nouns, stems of adjectives, and continuative (連用形) forms of verbs.

In certain contexts, this っぽい (-ppoi) may have overtones that the quality is inappropriate or unexpected, contrasting with らしい (-rashii) with overtones that the quality is appropriate and expected.
