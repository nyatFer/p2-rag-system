# っす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Wikipedia

Etymology

From です.

Verb

っす • (ssu)

contraction of です

Suffix

っす • (-ssu)

(colloquial, informal) informal polite marker, attaching to the non-polite form at the end of a sentence, ruder than regular polite forms です (desu) and ます (-masu)

知ってるっす。

Shitteru ssu.

I know.

俺のもんっす。

Ore no mon ssu.

This is mine.

すぐ来るっすよ。

Sugu kuru ssu yo.

It will come soon.

Usage notes

If the original non-polite sentence ends with だ (da), っす replaces だ.

Very colloquial and not considered standard. Often used by male students.
