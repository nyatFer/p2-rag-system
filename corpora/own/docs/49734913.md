Etymology 1

Various kanji compounds.

Noun

さんち • (sanchi)

Etymology 2

From French centimètre.

Noun

さんち • (sanchi)

サンチ: centimeter, especially of cannon caliber

Etymology 3

Contraction of さんのうち (-san no uchi).

Suffix

one's house
