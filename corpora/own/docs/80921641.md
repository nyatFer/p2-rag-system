Etymology 2

Contraction of ず (zu, negative continuative auxiliary) +‎ あり (ari, “to be, to exist”).

Suffix

ざり • (-zari) -ri

(archaic, Classical Japanese) not, un-

Usage notes

Attaches to the 未然形 (mizenkei, “irrealis form”) of verbs.
