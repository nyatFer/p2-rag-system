Unknown. First attested in the 1400s.

Noun

ざる or ザル • (zaru)

[from 1400s] A sieve, a colander, a strainer

(figurative) Something which has holes or imperfections in it

Someone who can drink a lot of alcohol

Etymology 2

Adnominal form of auxiliary verb suffix ざり (-zari, “un-, not”). Also analyzable as a contraction of negative auxiliary verb suffix ず (-zu) + copular verb あり (ari) in the adnominal conjugation form ある (aru).

Suffix

ざる • (-zaru) -ri

adnominal of ざり (zari, “not, un-”)

偽らざる気持ち ― itsuwarazaru kimochi ― true (unfeigned) feelings

こうならざるを得ない。 ― Kō narazaru o enai. ― It must be this way. (literally, “That it not be this way is impossible.”)
