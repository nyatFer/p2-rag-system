# ござる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

ござる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

There are two theories regarding the derivation:

A shift from 御座有る (gozaaru), a contraction of 御座 (goza, “noble seat”) + 有る (aru, “to have, to be”, literally “to have a noble seat”).

According to Kōjien, the above is derived from the Sino-Japanese reading of (the kanji in the ateji spelling of) 御座す (owasu) + 有り (ari) (the classical form of 有る (aru)).

Verb

ござる • (gozaru) intransitive godan (stem ござり (gozari) or ござい (gozai), past ござった (gozatta))

(intransitive, archaic) to exist, to be, to have

(auxiliary, polite) to be (used as a polite copula, typically following the particle で (de))

Usage notes

As an auxiliary verb and politeness marker, it is usually written in hiragana (ござる) in accordance with de facto modern orthographic standards, though it is not used in official documents (kōyōbun).

In classical Japanese, ござる (gozaru) was the polite form of 有る (aru) applicable to both animate and inanimate objects.

It survives in modern Japanese only in the inflected forms ございました (gozaimashita), ございましたら (gozaimashitara), ございます (gozaimasu), and です (desu) (which may originate from a contraction of でございます (de gozaimasu)).

In modern popular culture, it is associated with ninja.
