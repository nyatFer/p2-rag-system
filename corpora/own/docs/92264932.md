# 歩く (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

歩く (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From Old Japanese. First cited to the Man'yōshū of circa 759 CE.

Verb

歩く • (aruku) intransitive godan (stem 歩き (aruki), past 歩いた (aruita))

to walk

Synonym: 歩む (ayumu)

夜の街を歩く ― yoru no machi o aruku ― to walk the streets at night

私は歩いて行きます。 ― Watashi wa aruite ikimasu. ― I will go on foot.

湖まで歩いていきましょう。 ― Mizuumi made aruite ikimashō. ― Let's walk to the lake.

(auxiliary) to go around from place to place (doing something)

Synonym: 回る (mawaru)

訪ね歩く ― tazunearuku ― to visit various places
