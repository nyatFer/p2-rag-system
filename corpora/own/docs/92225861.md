# あて (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

あて (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Verb gerund.

Noun

あて • (ate)

aim; object; end

あてもなく歩く

ate mo naku aruku

to walk aimlessly

鳥を当てに鉄砲を撃つ

tori o ate ni teppō o utsu

to shoot a gun at a bird

行く宛もない

iku ate mo nai

with nowhere to go

hope; expectation; something to rely or depend upon

当てが外れる

ate ga hazureru

to be disappointed

当てにできる人

ate ni dekiru hito

a reliable person

pad; cushion; something added for protection

snack to have with drink

Suffix

あて • (-ate)

宛, 宛て: (addressed) to

学校あてに本を送る

gakkō ate ni hon o okuru

to send a book to the school

佐藤さんあての手紙

Satō-san ate no tegami

a letter addressed to Satō

(archaic) per

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana.

Etymology 2

Pronoun

あて • (ate)

(Kansai, feminine, dated) I; me
