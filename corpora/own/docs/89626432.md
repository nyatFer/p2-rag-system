# 以て (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

以て (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

From the gerund (-te form) of verb 持つ (motsu, “to hold”).

Compare Middle Korean ᄡᅥ (pse) / Korean 써 (sseo) from Middle Korean ᄡᅳ다 (psuta) / Korean 쓰다 (sseuda).

Conjunction

以て • (motte)
(preceded by を (o))

marks a method, process, materials by which something is done; with; by; by means of

marks a cause or reason; for; by

marks the time at which something occurs; in; at; on

Synonym: に (ni)

本日をもって閉店します。

Honjitsu o motte heiten shimasu.

We shall be closing the store today.

Verb

以て • (motte)

conjunctive form of 持つ (motsu) [godan]

Etymology 2

From もって (motte) above with the geminate consonant omitted.

Conjunction

以て • (mote)

with; by
