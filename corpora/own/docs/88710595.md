# てよ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

てよ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

て (te, conjunctive particle; sentence-ending particle of request) + よ (yo, particle indicating certainty, conviction, or emphasis)

Particle

てよ • (te yo)

(childish or women's speech) Indicates request or requirement in the spoiled manner: please

(chiefly women's speech) Indicates emphasis euphemistically with emotion.

Usage notes

When attached after the word ends with ん (n), it become into でよ (deyo).

When attached after the continuative form (連用形) of the adjective, it can change into ってよ (tteyo).

Etymology 2

Suffix

てよ • (-teyo)

(Classical Japanese) Imperative form (命令形) of つ (tsu), used in the intensive operative sentence.
