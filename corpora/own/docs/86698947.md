# とした (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

とした (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From と (to, “with”) + した (shita, perfective of する (to do)).

Used suppletively as the perfective form of たり (tari); compare similar use of related として (to shite) as its conjunctive form.

Particle

とした • (to shita)

perfective of たり (tari)

Usage notes

In modern use, used attributively with taru-adjectives to perfectively modify a noun. Compare the かった (-katta) form of i-adjectives.
