# ばかりか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ばかりか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From ばかり (bakari, “only”) + か (ka).

Particle

ばかりか • (bakari ka)

not only

祖父ばかりか、曽祖父さえ生きている。

Sofu bakari ka, sōsofu sae ikiteiru.

Not only is my grandfather living but my great-grandfather as well.

Usage notes

The term ばかりか (bakari ka) may be accompanied by さえ (sae) to indicate an unexpected occurrence.
