# 申す (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

申す (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

⟨mawosu⟩ → */mawusu/ → /mausu/ → /mɔːsu/ → /moːsu/

Shift from Old Japanese 申す (mawosu), Late Old Japanese mausu.

Verb

申す • (mōsu) transitive godan (stem 申し (mōshi), past 申した (mōshita))

(humble, formal) to say

(humble, formal) to be called

こちらは田中と申します。鈴木さんはおいででしょうか。

Kochira wa Tanaka to mōshimasu. Suzuki-san wa oide deshō ka.

This is Tanaka speaking. Is Mr. Suzuki there?

ジョンソンと申します。

Jonson to mōshimasu.

I am called Johnson.

(humble) to ask, request, or give thanks (to a kami or buddha)

(humble) to make a wish

(auxiliary) before a noun, usually with the honorific prefix 御 (o-/go-), to serve, perform an action for a superior

(auxiliary, archaic) attaches to 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem form”) of a verb: to formally or otherwise ceremonially do [verb]

Etymology 2

/mausu/ → */maːsu/ → /masu/

Thought to be a shift from mōsu above, historical mausu.

Verb

申す • (masu) transitive yodan

(rare, obsolete) Same as もうす (mōsu) above
