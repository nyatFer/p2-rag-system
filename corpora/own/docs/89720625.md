# てく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

てく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Suffix

てく • (-teku) godan

(colloquial) contraction of ていく (-te iku)

Usage notes

Attaches to the continuative form of a verb. It is conjugated irregularly like いく; the past and continuatives are conjugated as てった (-tetta) and てって (-tette).
