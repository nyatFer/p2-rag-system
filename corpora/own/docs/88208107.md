# ころ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ころ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

From Old Japanese, from Proto-Japonic *kərə.

Particle

ころ • (koro)

(time) in, on, at, at that time, around, about, approximately

三時ごろに会いましょう。

San-ji goro ni aimashō.

Let's meet at three o'clock.

昨日十二時ごろ寝ました。

Kinō jūni-ji goro nemashita.

I went to sleep at about twelve o'clock yesterday.

Usage notes

ころ (koro) functions as a noun and may be followed by other particles, such as の (no) and に (ni).

When used as a suffix, ごろ is used.

Noun

ころ • (koro)

such a time

time of year, season
