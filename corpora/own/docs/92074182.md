# 足す (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

足す (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Verb

足す • (tasu) transitive godan (stem 足し (tashi), past 足した (tashita))

to add

５と７を足すと１２になる。

Go to nana o tasu to jūni ni naru.

Add 5 and 7 and you get 12.

to take care of (business); to do (a task one needs to do)

Conjunction

足す • (tasu)

(arithmetic) plus; add

Synonym: プラス (purasu)

５足す５は１０だ。 ― Go tasu go wa jū da. ― 5 plus 5 is 10.
