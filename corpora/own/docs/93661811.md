# と (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

と (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 止 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

と • (to)

The hiragana syllable と (to). Its equivalent in katakana is ト (to). It is the twentieth syllable in the gojūon order; its position is た行お段 (ta-gyō o-dan, “row ta, section o”).

Etymology 2

⟨to2⟩ → */tə/ → /to/

From Old Japanese, from Proto-Japonic *tə.

Particle

と • (to)

(case-marking)

with; marks a companion, partner, etc. that an action is done with

僕と行きたい？ ― Boku to ikitai? ― Do you want to go with me?

友達と話していた。 ― Tomodachi to hanashite ita. ― I was talking with a friend.

can be optionally attached to some adverbs to (slightly) highlight the explanatory nature of the adverb

そよそよと風が吹く。 ― Soyosoyo to kaze ga fuku. ― The breeze blows gently.

marks the result of a transformation; (turn) to, into

Synonym: に (ni)

氷と化す ― kōri to kasu ― become, turn to ice

小川が合流して大河となる。

Ogawa ga gōryū shite taiga to naru.

Small streams merge and turn into large rivers.

follows a phrase or clause describing the detail, content or manner of an action; commonly used to mark dialogue or thoughts

「お願い！」と言った。 ― “Onegai!” to itta. ― "Please!" I said.

なんだと！？ ― Nan da to!? ― What!?

これはダメだと思います。 ― Kore wa dame da to omoimasu. ― I think this won't work.

(archaic, outside fixed expressions) when used with the same verb twice, used to mean "every"; constructed using the ren'yōkei first, followed by the rentaikei

ありとあらゆる ― ari to arayuru ― each and every

生きとし生けるもの ― iki to shi ikeru mono ― all living things

(parallel-marking) used to join nouns; and

それとこれ ― sore to kore ― that and this

英語とフランス語が話せる。

Eigo to furansugo ga hanaseru.

I can speak English and French.

犬と猫を私は飼っている。

Inu to neko o watashi wa katteiru.

I have the dog and the cat.

(conjunctive, attaches to a conjugable word in shūshikei) used to express co-occurrence or causality of events

marks an action, after which something else happens in succession; (soon) upon (doing something), when

マンハッタンへ行くと、大学の友達に会った。

Manhattan e iku to, daigaku no tomodachi ni atta.

When I went to Manhattan, I met a friend from university.

marks a condition which, whenever met, causes something to happen; whenever; if

二と三を掛けると六になる。

Ni to san o kakeru to roku ni naru.

If you multiply two and three, they make six.

marks a condition of which one wishes to express a reasonably expectable consequence; if

勉強すると分かる。 ― Benkyō suru to wakaru. ― If you study, you'll understand.

円高が進むと、日本経済が混乱する。

Endaka ga susumu to, Nihon keizai ga konran suru.

If the high yen continues, the Japanese economy will be in dire straits.

marks a short preface to something one is about to say

はっきり言うと、だめだ。 ― Hakkiri iu to, dame da. ― Put clearly, no.

Etymology 3

Ellipsis of すると (suru to), itself containing the conjunctive particle と (to, “upon”).

Conjunction

と • (to)

thus; thereupon

Etymology 4

Noun

と • (to)

short for と金 (tokin): a promoted 歩兵 (fuhyō, “pawn”) that moves like a 金将 (kinshō, “gold general”)

Etymology 5

Particle

と • (to)

(Kyūshū, final) indicates emphasis or a question

Synonyms: (standard) の, (Tosa) が

そんなことなかとよ。 ― Sonna koto naka to yo. ― Not at all.
