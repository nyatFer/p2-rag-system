# 所で (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

所で (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 所 (tokoro, “place”) +‎ で (de, “at”, “in”, indicates a location of action).

Conjunction

所で • (tokoro de)

by the way, incidentally

所で、ちょっと君に話が在る。

Tokoro de, chotto kimi ni hanashi ga aru.

By the way, I have something to tell you.

所で、あなたの仕事は何ですか？

Tokoro de, anata no shigoto wa nan desu ka?

By the way, what do you do?

Particle

所で • (tokoro de)

even if, no matter if (someone does something)
