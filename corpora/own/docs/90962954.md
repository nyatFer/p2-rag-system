# しめる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

しめる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Kana spelling of various terms.

Verb

しめる • (shimeru) transitive ichidan (stem しめ (shime), past しめた (shimeta))

絞める: to constrict

占める: to make up, to be a part of

締める, 〆る, 緊める: to fasten

閉める: to close

Verb

しめる • (shimeru) intransitive godan (stem しめり (shimeri), past しめった (shimetta))

湿る: to be wet

Etymology 2

Modern form of classical しむ (-shimu).

Suffix

しめる • (-shimeru) ichidan (stem しめ (-shime), past しめた (-shimeta))

(archaic, literary) used to make the causative form of verbs: to make; to let

言わしめる ― iwashimeru ― (modern language) to make [someone] say

中心地たらしめる ― chūshinchi-tarashimeru ― (modern language) to make a place a centre

満天下に知らしめる ― mantenka ni shirashimeru ― (modern language) to let the whole world know

Usage notes

This word is morphologically an inflectional suffix. It is classified as 助動詞 (jodōshi, “auxiliary verb”) in traditional Japanese grammar.
