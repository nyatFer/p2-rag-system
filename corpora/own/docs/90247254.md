# さて (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

さて (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Compare さして (sashite, “(not) to such an extent”), from 然 (sa), and さして (sashite, conjunctive form of さす (sasu)). For the latter, note readings さす (sasu) of kanji 扨 and 扠, also alternative spellings of this term.

Adverb

さて • (sate)

in that way

Conjunction

さて • (sate)

by the way, on the other hand

Interjection

さて • (sate)

well then, well, now

さて、これはどうやって使うんだ？

Sate, kore wa dō yatte tsukau nda?

Well then, how's this meant to be used?
