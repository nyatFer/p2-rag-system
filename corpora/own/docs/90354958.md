Etymology 1

Wikipedia

From English -ic in words ending in "-tic" like romantic.

Suffix

チック • (-chikku)

(colloquial) -tic, -ish (makes an adjectival noun)

乙女チックラブコメ

otome-chikku rabukome

girly rom-com; chick flick

洋楽チックな曲

yōgaku-chikku na kyoku

Western-style song

Noun
