# のみならず (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

のみならず (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From のみ (nomi, “only”) + ならず (narazu, negative form of なり (nari)).

Conjunction

のみならず • (nomi narazu)

as well as; not only … but also

ヨーロッパのみならず日本でも大ヒット

Yōroppa nomi narazu Nihon de mo dai-hitto

a smash hit not only in Europe, but also in Japan
