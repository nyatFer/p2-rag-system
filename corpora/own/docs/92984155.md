# る (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

る (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 留 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

る • (ru)

The hiragana syllable る (ru). Its equivalent in katakana is ル (ru). It is the forty-first syllable in the gojūon order; its position is ら行う段 (ra-gyō u-dan, “row ra, section u”).

Etymology 2

Extrapolated from the る (-ru) ending of many native verbs.

Suffix

る • (-ru) godan (stem り (-ri), past った (-tta))

(slang) general method to form 五段活用 (godan katsuyō) verbs

サボる

saboru

to skip, to play hooky; to slack off, to shirk one's duties

ググる

guguru

to google

Usage notes

Much less productive than する (-suru).

Etymology 3

Contraction of いる (iru).

Verb

る • (-ru) ichidan (stem る, past た (-ta))

(informal, used after the conjunctive て (-te) form of a verb as てる (-teru) or でる (-deru)) the present progressive: to be doing

待ってる

matteru

to be waiting

Etymology 4

See れる (-reru).

Suffix

る • (-ru) nidan

Classical Japanese form of れる (-reru, passive suffix)

Usage notes

Attaches to the 未然形 (mizenkei).

Etymology 5

Regular adnominal conjugation of verbal auxiliary suffix り (-ri) indicating resulting state or completion of action.

Suffix

る • (-ru)

(Classical Japanese or literary) adnominal of り (-ri)

眠れる森の美女

Nemureru Mori no Bijo

Sleeping Beauty

富める時も貧しき時も

tomeru toki mo mazushiki toki mo

(wedding vows) in wealth or poverty
