Etymology 1

An abbreviation for the kanji 箇 (ka), the counter for months, places or provisions.

Note that ヶ is a graphical abbreviation of the top of the character (𥫗, 竹), and is pronounced カ ka (or ガ ga), not as ケ ke. Compare simplified Chinese character 个.

Counter

ヶ or か • (ka)

箇: Counter for months, places or provisions of Classical Chinese origin.

三ヶ月 ― sanka getsu ― three months (in duration)

三ヶ国 ― sanka koku ― three countries

Counter

ヶ or こ • (ko)

個: General counter for inanimates.

３ヶ ― sanko ― three objects

Etymology 2

See etymology 1, compare Min Nan or Wu 個／个.

Particle

ヶ or が • (ga)

An alternative spelling of an obsolete possessive marker が (ga). Found only in place names and surnames derived from such.

藤ヶ丘 ― Fujigaoka ― a place name, literally "Hill of Wisteria"

四ヶ所麻美 ― Shikasho asami ― Asami Shikasho
