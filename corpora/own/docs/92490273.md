# ゆき (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

ゆき (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Noun

ゆき • (yuki)

for 大嘗祭 (Daijōsai), the country chosen to offer grain and alcohol

Etymology 2

Proper noun

ゆき • (Yuki)

a female given name

夕貴, 由姫: a surname
