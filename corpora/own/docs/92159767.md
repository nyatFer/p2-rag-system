# ながら (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ながら (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From Old Japanese, cited in the Man'yōshū poetry anthology of 759. In turn, analyzable as a compound of な (na, ancient genitive and possessive particle, apophonic form of particle の (no)) + 柄 (kara, “character, quality”).

Particle

ながら • (nagara)

(attaches to verbs in ren'yōkei) while, during, while doing (verb)

お茶を飲みながら話しましょう。

O-cha o nominagara hanashimashō.

Let's talk over tea.

(literally, “Let's talk while drinking tea.”)

歩きながら話しましょうか。

Arukinagara hanashimashō ka.

Shall we walk and talk?

(literally, “Shall we talk while walking?”)

(attaches to nouns, na-adjectives, verbs in ren'yōkei, adjectives) though, notwithstanding

残念ながら ― zannen nagara ― unfortunately (literally, “(it being a) regret notwithstanding”)

both (doing verb)

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana.
