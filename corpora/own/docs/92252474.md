# レス (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

レス (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Shortening of レスポンス (resuponsu), from English response.

Noun

レス • (resu)

(Internet) reply; response (to an electronic message); re

レスを返す

resu o kaesu

to write a reply; to reply

Verb

レスする • (resu suru) suru (stem レスし (resu shi), past レスした (resu shita))

(Internet) to reply; to respond

Etymology 2

English -less.

Suffix

レス • (-resu)

(colloquial) -less

Etymology 3

From English loess.

Noun

レス • (resu)

loess

Synonym: 黄土 (ōdo)

Etymology 4

Clipping of セックスレス (sekkusuresu, “sexless”).

Noun

レス • (resu)

(euphemistic) sexless, having no sexual activity for a long time (between a married couple)
