# よりによって (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

よりによって (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 選り (yori, 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 選る (yoru).) + に (ni) + 選って (yotte, conjunctive form of the verb 選る (yoru)).

Conjunction

よりによって • (yori ni yotte)

of all things, people, places, etc.

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana.

Used to lament a particularly undesirable outcome or choice.
