# まり (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

まり (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Noun

まり • (mari)

鞠: ball

Etymology 2

Readings of various personal names.

Proper noun

まり • (Mari)

茉莉, 麻里, 真梨, 真理: a female given name

Etymology 3

Clipping of 余り (amari), an archaism used to join Old Japanese numerals, e.g. as 十まり一つ ― tō mari hitotsu ― eleven (literally, “ten and one”).

Suffix

まり • (-mari)

(archaic) synonym of 余り, used to connect numerals: and
