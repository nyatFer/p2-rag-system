# とは (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

とは (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Particle

とは • (to wa)

indicates that a word or a phrase is to be defined or explained

素数とは何かを学んだ。

Sosū to wa nani ka o mananda.

I learnt what [the meaning of] a prime number was.

「原子力」とは？

“Genshiryoku” to wa?

What is [the meaning of] "nuclear power"?

(more emphatic) synonym of と (to, “with”)

あいつとはもう関係ない。

Aitsu to wa mō kankei nai.

I have nothing to do with them anymore.

過去とは違う自分

kako to wa chigau jibun

a self who is different from the [self of the] past

short for とは思わなかった (to wa omowanakatta, “did not think that ...”)

まさか自殺するとは……

Masaka jisatsu suru to wa……

I never would have thought that he/she would kill himself/herself...

Usage notes

This particle is often used, for example, in encyclopedias to define entries.

When used alone with a word it denotes the meaning of "this word is defined as?" and may be translated as "about" or "What is".
