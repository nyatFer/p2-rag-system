# 漁る (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

漁る (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

From Old Japanese. Attested in the Man'yōshū of circa 759 CE. From Proto-Japonic *asaru.

Verb

漁る • (asaru) transitive godan (stem 漁り (asari), past 漁った (asatta))

to look for food

to fish, to gather seafood

(informal) to dig through; to browse

人のSNSを漁る

hito no esu-enu-esu o asaru

to dig through someone's social media accounts

(auxiliary, attaches to a verb in ren'yōkei) to do here and there; to go around doing
