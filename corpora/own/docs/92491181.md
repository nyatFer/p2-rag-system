# や (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

や (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 也 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

や • (ya)

The hiragana syllable や (ya). Its equivalent in katakana is ヤ (ya). It is the thirty-sixth syllable in the gojūon order; its position is や行あ段 (ya-gyō a-dan, “row ya, section a”).

Etymology 2

Particle

や • (ya)

(parallel-marking) used to express a nonexhaustive list: and, or

お年寄りや体の不自由な方は登録しませんか。

O-toshiyori ya karada no fujiyū na kata wa tōroku shimasen ka.

Don’t seniors and the physically handicapped [and maybe others] register?

最近は犬や猫を飼っている人は多い。

Saikin wa inu ya neko o katte iru hito wa ōi.

These days, many people have dogs and cats [and maybe other animals].

(conjunctive) (immediately) upon

猫が私を見るや逃げた。

Neko ga watashi o miru ya nigeta.

The cat ran away upon seeing me.

(final)

used to emphasise an imperative or hortative

行こうや！ ― Ikō ya! ― Let's go!

はよせーや！ ― Hayo sē ya! ― (Kansai) Hurry up!

used to show indifference

まあ、いいや。 ― Mā, ii ya. ― Eh, whatever.

(interjectory)

placed after a name to call someone

used to emphasise certain adverbs

ましてや ― mashite ya ― much less; to say nothing of

さぞや ― sazo ya ― certainly; surely

Etymology 3

Phonetic change of じゃ (ja) in the late Edo period.

Verb

や • (ya)

(Fukuoka, Hokuriku, Kansai, Shikoku) to be

Synonyms: (standard) だ (da), (dialect) じゃ (ja)

人や。 ― Hito ya. ― It is a person.

どやった？ ― Do yatta? ― How was it?

大丈夫やないで。 ― Daijōbu yanai de. ― No, it's not okay.

まあ、ぼちぼちやなあ。

Mā, bochibochi ya nā.

Well, things are slow. [a stereotypical Ōsaka expression]

Usage notes

This word is phonologically enclitic, like particles, since it is basically a contraction of a particle, で (de), and a verb, ある (aru). It is classified as a 助動詞 (jodōshi, “auxiliary verb”) in traditional Japanese grammar.

Etymology 4

Adjective

や • (ya) -na (adnominal やな (ya na), adverbial やに (ya ni))

(informal) alternative form of いや (iya)

学校はやだ。 ― Gakkō wa ya da. ― I don't wanna go to school.
