# 引く (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

引く (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Verb

引く • (hiku) transitive or intransitive godan (stem 引き (hiki), past 引いた (hiita))

(transitive) to pull (something toward oneself)

(transitive) to pull (along); to drag; to drag along; to bring along while going somewhere

(transitive) to bring (people) in; to bring together

(transitive) to attract (someone's) attention

(transitive) to pull out; to stretch; to spread (something over a surface or a distance)

線を引く ― sen o hiku ― to draw a line

フライパンに油をひく ― furaipan ni abura o hiku ― to add oil to a frying pan

(transitive) to subtract; to take away

６から４を引くと２になる。

Roku kara yon o hiku to ni ni naru.

Subtract 4 from 6 and you get 2.

(transitive) to catch (an illness)

風邪を引いちゃった。

Kaze o hiichatta.

I caught a cold.

(transitive) to page through (a dictionary); to look up

知らない単語は辞書を引いてください。

Shiranai tango wa jisho o hīte kudasai.

Please look up words you don't know in a dictionary.

(transitive) to draw (lots etc.)

くじを引く ― kuji o hiku ― to draw lots

ガチャを引く ― gacha o hiku ― to pull from a gacha

(intransitive) to pull out; to pull back; to withdraw; to retreat

(intransitive) to subside; to abate; to weaken

痛みが引く ― itami ga hiku ― pain subsides

熱が引く ― netsu ga hiku ― a fever goes down

(intransitive) to draw away from a person; to find someone repulsive to be turned off

Synonym: なえる (naeru)

あいつみたいな人は引くわ。

Aitsu mitai na hito wa hiku wa.

I'd totally stay far away from that kind of person.

好きな人に引かれた

suki na hito ni hikareta

got avoided by my crush

Conjunction

引く • (hiku)

(mathematics) minus (subtraction)

１２引く６は６だ。

Jūni hiku roku wa roku da.

12 minus 6 is 6.
