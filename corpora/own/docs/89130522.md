# だらけ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

だらけ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Suffix

だらけ • (-darake)

covered with

服が泥だらけ！

Fuku ga doro-darake!

My clothes are covered with mud.

full of; having many

穴だらけ ― ana-darake ― full of holes

Usage notes

Attaches to nouns.

This term often has negative connotations.
