# 上に (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

上に (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Conjunction

上に • (ue ni)

(following attributive) in addition to; as well as

その上に ― sono ue ni ― in addition to that; moreover

気前がいい上に男前だ。

Kimae ga ii ue ni otokomae da.

I am generous, as well as handsome.

Used other than figuratively or idiomatically: see 上,‎ に. ("above; on top of")
