# 置く (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

置く (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Verb

置く • (oku) godan (stem 置き (oki), past 置いた (oita))

to put, place, set

俺の机には置くな。

Ore no tsukue ni wa oku na.

Don't even think about putting it on my desk!

置く, 擱く: to put down (a tool) (and thereby stop doing the task the tool was for)

筆を擱く ― fude o oku ― to stop writing (literally, “to put down the brush”)

to leave (something)

私を一人にしておいてください。

Watashi o hitori ni shite oite kudasai.

Please leave me alone.

to house; to put up; to take in (to let (someone) stay at one's own house)

to leave (space)

距離をおいて歩く二人

kyori o oite aruku futari

two people walking with distance between them

(auxiliary) [with (conjunctive +) て]

to do something in advance as preparation

今晩凍り付くかもしれませんので鉢植えを玄関に移しておきます。

Konban kōritsuku ka mo shiremasen no de hachiue o genkan ni utsushite okimasu.

Since it might freeze this evening, I'll move the potted plants into the foyer.

to do and keep in that state

目を開けておくのよ。 ― Me o akete oku no yo. ― Keep your eyes open.

Usage notes

(sense 6): This term is often spelled in hiragana, as おく (oku).
