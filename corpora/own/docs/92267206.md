# たって (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

たって (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

A shift from たとて (-ta tote), a combination of た (-ta, suffix showing completion/realization of an action) + とて (tote, “even if”, a conditional particle, itself being a compound of quotative particle と (to) and conjunctive particle て (te)).

Surface analysis た (-ta) + って (tte).

Suffix

たって • (-tatte)

even if …

言ったって何も変わらない

ittatte nani mo kawaranai

even if I say it, nothing will change

好きじゃなくたっていい

suki ja nakutatte ii

it's okay if you don't like me

Usage notes

Attaches exactly like た (-ta), which see for details.

Similarly voices to だって (-datte) when attached to a godan verb ending in -bu, -gu, -mu, or -nu.
