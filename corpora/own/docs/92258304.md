# なば (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

なば (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Compound of な (na, the 未然形 (mizenkei, “irrealis form”) of ぬ (nu, particle of perfect tense)) +‎ ば (ba, conditional suffix).

First cited in the Man'yōshū of 759.

Suffix

なば • (-naba)

[from 759] (Classical Japanese) used for anticipating and assuming how something goes when the matter is completed

[from 759] (Classical Japanese) used to state the speaker's expectation if something were the case certainly

Etymology 2

According to one theory, a shortening of 蘖 (nabae, “a bud growing from a tree stump”). , or possibly related to 鍋 (nabe, “cooking pot”).

Cognate with Kunigami なばー (nabā, “mushroom”), Okinawan なーば (nāba, “mushroom”), Miyako なば (naba, “dirt, filth; scurf on body skin”), Yonaguni なば (naba, “mushroom”). Accentual comparison supports the reconstruction of Proto-Japonic *nanpa (accent class 2.4a).

First cited to the Myōgoki of 1275.

Noun

なば • (naba)

[from 1275] 茸: (dialectal, Chūgoku, Kyūshū, western part of Shikoku) mushroom

Synonym: 茸 (kinoko)

今晩、なば煮ちゃろういね。

Konban, naba nicharōine.

Tonight, I'm a-goin' to boil mushrooms for ye. (Shimonoseki dialect)

くそなば ― kusonaba ― (Hiroshima dialect): inedible mushrooms (literally, “shitty mushrooms”)
