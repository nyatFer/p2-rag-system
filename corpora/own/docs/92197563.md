# さえ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

さえ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

From 添へ (sohe), 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of classical verb 添ふ (sofu, modern equivalent 添える (soeru)).

Particle

さえ • (sae)

(emphatic) even

犬さえ食わない。 ― Inu sae kuwanai. ― Not even a dog would eat that.

(in a conditional phrase in ば (-ba) or たら (-tara)) as long as

アキちゃんさえいればなんでもできる。

Aki-chan sae ireba nandemo dekiru.

I can do anything as long as Aki-chan is there.

Etymology 2

Proper noun

さえ • (Sae)

a female given name

宮川紗江

Miyakawa Sae

(name of a gymnast)

Etymology 3

Verb

さえ • (sae)

imperfective and stem (or continuative) forms of さえる (saeru) [ichidan]
