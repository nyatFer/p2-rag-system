# につき (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

につき (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Particle

につき • (ni tsuki)

about (in concern with)

工事中につき要注意

kōji-chū ni tsuki yōchūi

must be aware of ongoing construction

(with units) per

一週間につき

isshūkan ni tsuki

for every week

Etymology 2

Verb

につき • (nitsuki)

stem or continuative form of につく (nitsuku) [godan]
