# かて (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

かて (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Contraction of 糧 (karite, “provisions for a journey”), with the meaning changing to mean food generally. The initial kari is a shift from 餉 (karei, historical karefi), from earlier 乾飯 (kareī, “cooked and dried rice”, historical kareifi). The origin of the final -te is unclear, but is considered to be a synonym of 料 (ryō, “materials; ingredients”).

Noun

かて • (kate)

food

(figurative) guiding material for future endeavors

人生の糧となる

jinsei no kate to naru

to become a guiding force in one's life

Etymology 2

Shift from かてて (katete), itself a shift from かとて (katote), a compound of か (ka, suppositional particle) + とて (tote, conditional particle).

Particle

かて • (kate)

(archaic or Kansai, conjunctive) even if

Synonyms: からとて, にしても

そんな事言うたかて、出来へんもんは出来へんねん。

No matter what you say, what's impossible is impossible. (lit. Even if you say that kind of thing, things that can't [happen], can't [happen]!)

(Kansai, binding) even

Synonym: (standard) だって
