# まがい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

まがい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

The 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb まがう (magau, “to be mistaken for, to be confused with”).

Noun

まがい • (magai)

fake, imitation

Suffix

まがい • (-magai)

resembling

Verb

まがい • (magai)

stem or continuative form of まがう (magau) [godan]
