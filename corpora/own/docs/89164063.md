麻呂

Wikipedia

Likely a compound of ま (ma) and suffix ろ (-ro), similar to pronoun わろ (waro) from 我 (wa) and ろ (-ro).

Another hypothesis derives it from a meaning "chap, fellow".

Pronoun

まろ • (maro)

(archaic) I, me, my, mine

Suffix

まろ • (-maro)

(obsolete) used in male names
