# ばかし (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ばかし (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Change in pronunciation from ばかり (bakari).

Particle

ばかし • (bakashi)

just, full of, only, always

Usage notes

Generally only used in verbal speech. Seldom used in writing. May be considered dialectal.

The kanji form 許し is less commonly used.
