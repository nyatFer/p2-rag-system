# やす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

やす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Noun

やす • (yasu)

簎, 矠: spear, lance

Proper noun

やす • (Yasu)

靖: a female given name

Suffix

やす • (-yasu)

(dated, colloquial) contraction of ます

Usage notes

This form of denasalisation is also seen in すいません (suimasen) for 済みません.
