# てしまう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

てしまう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

て +‎ しまう.

Suffix

てしまう • (-te shimau)

indicates that something undesirable was done or happened

そして彼は死んでしまいました。

Soshite kare wa shinde shimaimashita.

And so he died.

Usage notes

てしまう is used by attaching しまう to a verb in its て form.
