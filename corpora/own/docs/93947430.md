# め (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

め (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 女 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

め • (me)

The hiragana syllable め (me). Its equivalent in katakana is メ (me). It is the thirty-fourth syllable in the gojūon order; its position is ま行え段 (ma-gyō e-dan, “row ma, section e”).

Etymology 4

First cited to The Tale of the Heike from the early 1300s.

Might be a shift in usage of 目 (me, “visually prominent feature”). The 奴 spelling does not appear to be common, and this is not included in all dictionaries.

Suffix

め • (-me)

(derogatory) attaches to names or words referring to people, animals, etc. to show contempt [from early 1300s]

ばかめ！ ― Baka-me! ― You idiot!

(humble) attaches to words referring to oneself or one's things

私めをお許しください。 ― Watashi-me o o-yurushi kudasai. ― Please forgive me.
