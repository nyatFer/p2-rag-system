Wikipedia

Likely comes from こなす (konasu, “to break down (earth); to break to pieces (earth)”) as a trowel tool. The kanji spellings appear to be ateji (当て字).

This term appears to be rare, and it is not listed in many dictionaries.

Noun

くない or クナイ • (kunai)

(weaponry) a Japanese weapon, possibly derived from a masonry trowel

Etymology 2

Derived as a repurposing of the negative ending -ku nai of regular -i adjectives. Appears as slang in informal usage where standard Japanese would use じゃない (ja nai) instead.

Particle
