# ぽち (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

ぽち (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Noun

ぽち or ポチ • (pochi)

a small spot or protrusion; speck, point, dot

Synonyms: ぽっち (potchi), ぽつ (potsu), ちょぼ (chobo)

(Kansai) gratuity, tip

Synonyms: 心付け (kokorozuke), 祝儀 (shūgi), チップ (chippu)

Suffix

ぽち • (-pochi)

synonym of ぽっち (-potchi)

Etymology 2

There are various theories about the derivation:

English spotty

English pooch

French petit (“small, little, petty”)

Proper noun

ぽち or ポチ • (Pochi)

a popular dog name
