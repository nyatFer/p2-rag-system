# いは (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

いは (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

ipa → iɸa → iwa. Compound of い (i, “emphatic marker”) and は (wa, “focus marker”). As -i was archaic even in Old Japanese, -wa was attached to supplement it.

Particle

いは • (iwa)
