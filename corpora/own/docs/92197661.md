# なく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

なく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Regular adverbial form of ない (nai), when used as either a verb suffix or as the plain negative of ある (aru).

Adverb

なく • (naku)

adverbial/continuative of ない (nai)

Etymology 2

The reading of various verbs.

Etymology 3

Inherited from Old Japanese. The -ku nominal form of ず (-zu, negation auxilliary, adnominal form ぬ (-nu)). Old Japanese phonological rules cause the contraction of vowel cluster -ua- into -a-.

/-nu aku/ → /-naku/

Suffix

なく • (-naku)

(Classical Japanese) nominalization of ず (auxilliary expressing negation)
