# なんで (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

なんで (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Adverb

なんで • (nande)

why?

(transport) how? by what means?

なんでここに来ましたか。

Nande koko ni kimashita ka.

How did you get here? (by walking? driving? etc...)

Usage notes

Chiefly informal language. Although usage of なんで in 丁寧語 does not necessarily violate the register, it has a casual tone, making どうして preferable in most polite contexts.

Etymology 2

Particle

なんで • (nande)

(informal) contraction of なので (na no de)
