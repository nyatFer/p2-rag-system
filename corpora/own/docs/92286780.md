# なのに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

なのに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Compound of particle な (na, attributive form (連体形) of auxiliary verb だ (da) or copula particle used after the adjective verbs (形容動詞)) and のに (noni, particle of contrastive conjunction).

Phrase

なのに • (na no ni)

but, in spite of, however, although

Etymology 2

Clipped from それなのに (sore na noni, “even so, nevertheless”).

Conjunction

なのに • (na noni)

Used to receive the previous sentence and continue in the contrastive conjunction: nevertheless, even so, still, and yet
