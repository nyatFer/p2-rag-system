# あー (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

あー (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Probably borrowed from English -er, sometime around 1994.

Suffix

あー • (-ā)

Used to denote fanatics, adepts or devotees of a certain product, artist, fashion style or famous person

‎シャネル (“Chanel”) + ‎あー (-ā) → ‎シャネラー (“someone who likes to use Chanel products”)

‎浜田 (“Hamada”) + ‎あー (-ā) → ‎ハマダー (“a fan of Masatoshi Hamada, of his style”)
