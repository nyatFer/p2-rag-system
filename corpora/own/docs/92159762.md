# どん (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

どん (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Noun

どん • (don)

A sound made by one object hitting another. Thud; bang; thump.

A word used to indicate the start of a race or competition.

Etymology 2

Shift from 殿 (-dono, honorific suffix).

Suffix

どん • (-don)

(Kagoshima, honorific, dialect) synonym of さん (-san)

Etymology 3

Strengthened form of ど (do-, “very; super”, intensifying prefix).

Prefix

どん • (don-)

extremely; super
