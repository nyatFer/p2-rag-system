# てば (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

てば (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

て (te, continuative form (連用形) of つ (tsu)) + ば (ba, particle forming the provisional).

Phrase

てば • (te ba)

(archaic, obsolete) Resultative assumption: if

Etymology 2

Contraction of と言えば (to ieba), from と (to, particle of quotation) + 言え (ie, hypothetical form (仮定形) of 言う (iu, “to say”)) + ば (ba, particle forming the provisional).

Particle

てば • (teba)

(informal, rare) contraction of と言えば (to ieba): speaking of

(informal, childish or women's speech) Binding particle used to show as a topic with feelings of familiarity or criticism.

うちの父さんてば足が臭いのよ。

Uchi no tōsan teba ashi ga kusai noyo.

My daddy does have stinky legs.

(informal) Sentence-final particle used to emphasize a point and express irritation.

そんな事はしませんてば。

Sonna koto wa shimasen teba.

I'm not going to do such a thing.

Usage notes

Attaches only after the words end with ん (n). Otherwise, it changes into ってば (tteba).

Etymology 3

Noun

てば • (teba)

手羽: chicken wing
