# ばかり (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ばかり (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Shift from noun 計り, 測り, 量り (hakari, “measurement, amount, full amount”), the 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “continuative or stem form”) of verb 計る, 測る, 量る (hakaru, “to measure, to weigh, to calculate”). The meaning seems to have shifted from amount to fully, and from there to just or only.

Particle

ばかり • (bakari)
(adverbial)

just; only; nothing but

Synonyms: だけ (dake), のみ (nomi)

謎は深まるばかりだ。

Nazo wa fukamaru bakari da.

The mystery only deepens.

今回ばかりはよくやった。

Konkai bakari wa yoku yatta.

Nothing but well done this time.

(in the form 〜てばかりいる (~te bakari iru)) only, always (doing something)

彼は食べてばかりいる。

Kare wa tabete bakari iru.

He's always eating.

落ち込んでばかりいずに前に進もう。

Ochikonde bakari izu ni mae ni susumō.

Take action instead of moping all day.

full of nothing but [with a noun]

東京は人ばかりだ。

Tōkyō wa hito bakari da.

Tokyo is just full of people.

just (recently completed action) [with past verb]

食べたばかりだ。 ― Tabeta bakari da. ― I just ate.

ばかり, 許り: roughly, about [with a number]
