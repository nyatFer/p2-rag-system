# ニキ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

ニキ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 兄貴 (aniki, “elder brother”).

Suffix

ニキ • (-niki)

(2channel slang, Internet slang) someone strong and dependable, a brother-in-arms

海外ニキ ― kaigai-niki ― my overseas bro

パソコン自信ニキ ― pasokon jishin niki ― computer knower

ピアニストニキ ― pianisutoniki ― pianist bro
