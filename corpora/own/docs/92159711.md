# こなす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

こなす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Verb

こなす • (konasu) godan (stem こなし (konashi), past こなした (konashita))

to digest

Synonym: 消化する (shōka suru)

to break down; to break to pieces

to deal with (well); to manage; to finish

Suffix

こなす • (-konasu)

well, skilfully

Usage notes

Attach after the 連用形 (ren'yōkei) form of a verb. This term is often spelled in kana.
