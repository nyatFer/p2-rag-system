# 御座ります (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

御座ります (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 御座り (gozari, 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 御座る (gozaru, “to be; to exist; to come or go”, respectful form)) + ます (masu, honorific auxiliary).

Verb

御座ります • (gozarimasu)

(honorific, referring to others) to go; to come; to be in a place

Synonym: いらっしゃいます (irasshaimasu)

polite form of ある (aru): to exist

Synonym: ございます (gozaimasu)

(auxiliary) polite form of 御座る (gozaru)

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana, as ござります (gozarimasu).
