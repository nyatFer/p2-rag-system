# にゃ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

にゃ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Syllable

にゃ • (nya)

The hiragana syllable にゃ (nya). Its equivalent in katakana is ニャ (nya).

Etymology 2

Onomatopoeic

Noun

にゃ • (nya)

(onomatopoeia) mew, meow, miaow

にゃあと鳴く

nyā to naku

to mew

Etymology 3

Particle

にゃ • (nya)

contraction of には (ni wa)
