Etymology 1

Ultimately from Egyptian rꜥ.

Proper noun

ラー • (Rā)

(Egyptian mythology) Ra, the sun god

Synonym: レー (Rē)

Etymology 2

Ultimately from English -er, as a reanalysis of the suffix from シャネラー (shanerā, “a "Chaneler": someone who loves Chanel”), shifting from the expected ā from English -er, to also include the consonant as rā, in accordance with Japanese phonological rules.
