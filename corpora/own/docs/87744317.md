Etymology

Back-formation from ツンデレ (tsundere) and ヤンデレ (yandere), themselves blends from でれでれ (deredere).

Suffix

デレ • (-dere)

(fandom slang, Japanese fiction) character (usually female) with a certain way of interacting with their love interest

personality and manners of such a character
