# 加うるに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

加うるに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Classical attributive (rentaikei) form of 加える (kuwaeru, “to add”) + に (ni).

Conjunction

加うるに • (kuwauru ni)

besides, furthermore
