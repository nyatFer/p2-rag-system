# アナ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

アナ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Clipping of アナウンサー (anaunsā, “announcer”), itself a borrowing from English announcer.

Appears in texts from 1933.

Noun

アナ • (ana)

clipping of アナウンサー (anaunsā, “a media announcer, a TV or radio presenter”)

Suffix

アナ • (-ana)

Honorific used for news announcers.

山田アナ

Yamada-ana

Announcer Yamada

Etymology 2

Clipping of either アナーキスト (anākisuto), from English anarchist, or アナーキズム (anākizumu), from English anarchism.

Appears in texts from 1948.

Noun

アナ • (ana)

clipping of アナーキスト (anākisuto, “an anarchist”)

clipping of アナーキズム (anākizumu, “anarchism”)

アナ・ボル論争

Ana Boru Ronsō

the Anarchist–Bolshevist dispute (a political controversy in Japan in 1922)
