# ばっか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ばっか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Shift from ばかり (bakari); considering the phonology, might more specifically be from emphatic variant ばっかり (bakkari).

First attested in volume six of 続膝栗毛 (Zoku Hiza Kurige, “Travels on Foot, Continued”), a series of comedy novels published from 1810 through 1822.

Particle

ばっか • (bakka)

[from early 1800s] (informal, conversational) partial synonym of ばかり (bakari), with the meaning narrowed:

in a limiting sense: only, just, no more than

in a time sense: just (happened)
