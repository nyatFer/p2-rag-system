# つけ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

つけ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 付ける (tsukeru, “attach; affix”).

Verb

つけ • (tsuke)

stem or continuative form of 付ける (tsukeru)

Noun

つけ or ツケ • (tsuke)

bill

account, credit

paying on credit

figuratively, consequences

ツケ: (go) contact move

Prefix

つけ • (tsuke-)

attached, fixed

external, external medicine

false, false mustache, sham

Suffix

つけ • (-tsuke)

one's favorite, accustomed
