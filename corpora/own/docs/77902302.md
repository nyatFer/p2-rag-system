# んち (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

んち (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Contraction of のうち.

Suffix

んち • (-nchi)

ん家: one's house

佐藤んち ― Satō-nchi ― Sato's house

俺んち ― ore-nchi ― my home

Etymology 2

Contraction of 日 (nichi)

Suffix

んち • (-nchi)

日: day (of the month), counter for days

一日 ― ichinchi ― one day

三月十七んち ― sangatsu jūnananchi ― March 17th
