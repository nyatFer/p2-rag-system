# ものか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ものか (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 物 (mono, “thing”) + か (ka, interrogative particle).

Particle

ものか • (mono ka)

(after attributive forms, at the end of a sentence) Expressing strong negation: definitely not; how can it be possible

黙っていられるものか！

Damatte irareru mono ka!

How can I shut up on this!

子供のものか！

Kodomo no mono ka!

I am not a child!
