# けど (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

けど (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Clipping of けれど (keredo, “although, but”), itself a clipping of older and longer form けれども (keredomo).

First attested in 1732.

Conjunction

けど • (kedo)

[from 1809] informal form of けれども (keredomo)

けどさあ、よく考えてみたらキモくね？

Kedo sā, yoku kangaete mitara kimoku ne?

But when you think about it though, isn't it kinda disgusting?

Particle

けど • (kedo)

[from 1732] informal form of けれども (keredomo)

彼女は一風変わっているけど綺麗だ。

Kanojo wa ippū kawatte iru kedo kirei da.

She is strange but pretty.

痛いけど気持ちいい。

Itai kedo kimochi ii.

It hurts but it feels good.
