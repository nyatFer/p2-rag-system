# かえ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

かえ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 替える (kaeru, “substitute, replace”), 代える, or 換える.

Noun

かえ or 替え or 代え or 換え • (kae)

substitute, proxy

change, alteration

替え: replacement

換え: exchange rate

Etymology 2

Compound of か (ka, interrogative particle) + え (e, exclamatory particle)

Particle

かえ • (kae)

(dated) interrogative particle
