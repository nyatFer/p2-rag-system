# たろう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

たろう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Compound of たら (tara-, 未然形 (mizenkei) of たり (tari, perfective auxiliary verb, whence modern past tense suffix た (-ta))) + う (u, conjectural suffix), with a regular /au/ → /oː/ vowel shift. A synchronic analysis may view this a contraction of た (-ta, past tense suffix) + だろう (darō, conjectural form of copula だ (da)).

Suffix

たろう • (-tarō)

shows conjecture about a past or completed action ("Wasn't it [...]?", "Didn't I [...]?")

言ったろう？

Ittarō?

Didn't I tell you so?

Usage notes

Takes the form だろう (darō) following -bu, -gu, -mu, and -nu godan verbs.
