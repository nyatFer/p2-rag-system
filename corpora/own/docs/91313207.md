# 下さい (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

下さい (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Imperative (命令形 (meireikei)) form of 下さる (kudasaru, “to give”), the honorific form of 呉れる (kureru). This form is said to have either directly shifted from an earlier 下され (kudasare), which is more typical of 四段活用 (yodan katsuyō, “quadrigrade inflection”) and its descendant 五段活用 (godan katsuyō, “quintigrade inflection”), or to be a clipping of 下さいまし (kudasaimashi) or 下さいませ (kudasaimase), from 下さり (kudasari, 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 下さる (kudasaru), then shifting from kudasari to kudasai in a process called イ音便 (i-onbin, “-i sound shift”)) + まし (-mashi) or ませ (-mase), imperative forms of ます (-masu).

Verb

下さい • (kudasai)
(polite)

imperative form of 下さる (kudasaru)

(with a noun) please give (me or us)

領収書を下さい。 ― Ryōshūsho o kudasai. ― Please give me a receipt.

手紙をください。 ― Tegami o kudasai. ― Please send me a letter.

(auxiliary, with a verb in て (-te) form or a verbal noun prefixed with お (o) or ご (go)) please [verb], please do [verb] (for me or us)

ゆっくり話して下さい。 ― Yukkuri hanashite kudasai. ― Please speak slowly.

計画をご検討ください。 ― Keikaku o go-kentō kudasai. ― Please consider the plan. (honorific)

現地に着いたら電話して下さい。

Genchi ni tsuitara denwa shite kudasai.

Please call me when you get there.

stem or continuative form of 下さる (kudasaru) [godan]

Usage notes

The auxiliary sense is attached either to the て (-te) form of a verb, or to a verbal stem or noun prefixed with お (o-) or ご (go-).

According to standard Japanese orthographic conventions, auxiliary verbs (補助動詞 (hojo dōshi)) are written in hiragana. Therefore, the kanji spelling 下さい is prescriptively restricted to its use as an independent verb meaning “to give” with a noun argument, such as when requesting an object. When functioning as an auxiliary suffix to express a request for an action (e.g. following a verb in the て (-te) form, or a verbal stem/noun prefixed with お (o-) or ご (go-)), it is written in kana.
