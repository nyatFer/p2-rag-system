# くさい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

くさい (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From Old Japanese. First attested in the Nihon Shoki of 720 logographically as 臰, and in the Shin'yaku Kegonkyō Ongi Shiki of 794 as 久佐之. In turn, from Proto-Japonic *kusa. Cognate with 糞 (kuso, “feces, arse, shit”) and 腐る (kusaru, “to rot”).

Adjective

くさい • (kusai) -i (adverbial くさく (kusaku))

stinking, smelly, pungent; giving off a bad smell

suspicious, fishy; arousing suspicion

Suffix

くさい • (-kusai)

smells like; reeks of

鉄臭い ― tetsu-kusai ― metallic, like blood

(negatively) very much like; conceptually similar to

ジジ臭い ― jiji-kusai ― like an old man

昭和臭い ― Shōwa-kusai ― like the Shōwa era

(with a na-adjective stem) an intensifier

面倒臭い ― mendō-kusai ― a hassle

Usage notes

Forms an i-adjective.
