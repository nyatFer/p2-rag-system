# けく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

けく (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From the contraction of final き (ki) in the 連体形 (rentaikei) forms of ク活用 (ku-katsuyō) and シク活用 (shiku-katsuyō) adjectives with earlier 所 (aku).

Suffix

けく • (-keku)

synonym of く (“nominalization suffix”)

Usage notes

This form is used after the basic non-inflected stem of ク活用 (ku-katsuyō) and シク活用 (shiku-katsuyō) adjectives; e.g. 安けく (yasukeku), 悲しけく (kanashikeku).
