# だけ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

だけ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived from 丈 (take, “height, limit”). Usage as a particle appears after 1600, during the Edo period.

Particle

だけ • (dake)

in a negative, limiting sense: only, just, limit

Synonyms: ばかり (bakari), のみ (nomi), しか (shika)

ローマ字だけの辞書

Rōmaji dake no jisho

rōmaji-only dictionary

in a positive, non-limiting sense: amount, as much as

Synonyms: ほど (hodo), くらい (kurai), 限り (kagiri)

寝たいだけ寝ていい。

Netai dake nete ii.

You can sleep as much as you want.

これだけしてるのに

kore dake shiteru no ni

Even though I'm doing as much as this / Even though I'm doing this much

Usage notes

This particle is exclusively written in kana in modern usage.
