# 合わせて (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

合わせて (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

The -te conjunctive conjugation of verb 合わせる (awaseru, “to bring or put together”).

Adverb

合わせて • (awasete)

together with, at the same time as (whatever came before)

all together, in total

Conjunction

合わせて • (awasete)

in addition, besides
