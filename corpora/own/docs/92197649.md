# とる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

とる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Ultimately cognate with 手 (te, “hand”).

Verb

とる • (toru) transitive godan (stem とり (tori), past とった (totta))

取る: to take

人の手を取る ― hito no te o toru ― to take someone by the hand

取る: (board games) to capture

取る: to pick up

花束を手に取る ― hanataba o te ni toru ― to pick up a bouquet of flowers

取る: to pick up on; to grasp

意味を取る ― imi o toru ― to grasp the meaning

バランスを取る ― baransu o toru ― to achieve balance

バランスが取れない ― baransu ga torenai ― unable to maintain balance

曲の拍子が取れない ― kyoku no hyōshi ga torenai ― unable to pick up on the time of a song

取る: to earn

いい成績をとる ― ii seiseki o toru ― to get good grades

取る, 脱る: to remove, take off (clothing, accessories, etc.)

Synonym: 脱ぐ (nugu)

眼鏡を取る ― megane o toru ― to take off one's glasses

ブラジャーを取る ― burajā o toru ― to take off one's bra

取る: to remove

歯の黄ばみを取る ― ha no kibami o toru ― to remove the yellowness from teeth

撮る, 録る: to photograph, film, or record; to take a picture

虫を撮る ― mushi o toru ― to take pictures of bugs

火のあかりを写真に撮ったりしていました。

Hi no akari o shashin ni tottari shiteimashita.

They were taking photos of the light of the flame.

捕る, 獲る, 取る, 把る: to catch; to capture

虫を取る ― mushi o toru ― to catch bugs

獲る: to hunt (animals)

盗る, 取る, 奪る: to steal

執る: to do (work), to perform (duties), to take (trouble)

執る: to take the command

摂る, 取る: to have, to eat (food, a meal)

朝食を摂る ― chōshoku o toru ― to have breakfast

摂る: to take nutrients, to take in

採る, 取る: to take, to adopt (a measure, a choice, etc.), to choose

執る: to take, to adopt, to assume (an attitude, manner of behaviour)

傲慢な態度をとる ― gōman na taido o toru ― to assume a haughty attitude

採る: to take (a blood sample etc.)

採る, 取る: to pick (plants), to harvest

採る: to employ

Etymology 2

Suffix

とる • (-toru) godan (stem とり (-tori), past とった (-totta))

(informal) contraction of ておる (-te oru)
