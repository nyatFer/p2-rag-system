# たら (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

たら (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Particle

たら • (tara)

follows a past-tense subordinate clause of condition; if, when

after doing something

Usage notes

Attaches to verbs and adjectives in the same way as た (-ta).

This word is morphologically an inflectional suffix. In traditional Japanese grammar, it is treated as the kateikei of the auxiliary verb た (-ta).

For the differences between たら (tara) and なら (nara), please see なら (nara).

Etymology 2

From Old Japanese. Attested in Shosōin documents spelled variously as 多羅, 太良, and 太羅. In turn, possibly borrowed from an ancestor of Korean 달 (dal, “reed”).

Already fell into disuse sometime after the Heian period, as the last dictionaries to mention this was the Shinsen Jikyō of 898-901, and the Wamyō Ruijushō of 934.

Noun

たら • (tara)

(obsolete) knotweed

Synonym: 蓼 (tade)
