# とって (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

とって (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Sound shift from 取りて (torite).

Conjunction

とって • (totte)

(when calculating age) to attain

当年とって18歳

tōnen totte jūhassai

He attained the age of 18 this year/He became 18 this year.

in the pattern of にとって, for, regarding, as far as ... is concerned

誰にとっても無駄だ。

Dare ni totte mo muda da.

No good for anybody.

Etymology 2

Sound shift from とて by gemination, usually informal.
Shortening of various phrases such as といって, と思って (to omotte), といっても, and としても.
