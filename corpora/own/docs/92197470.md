# おし (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

おし (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

The 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “continuative form”) of the verbs 押す (osu, “to push”), 推す (osu, “to infer; to support”), and 圧す (osu, “to press”). These verbs represent a single native Japanese word descending from Proto-Japonic *əsu, with different kanji utilized to distinguish specific semantic nuances.

Noun

おし • (oshi)

押し: pushing

押し: persuasion (especially in terms of romance)

押しに弱い人

oshi ni yowai hito

a person who is easily hit on

推し: (idol fandom) favorite idol

Suffix

おし • (-oshi)

推し: (idol fandom) being a devoted fan of …; … stan

Verb

おし • (oshi)

押し, 圧し, 推し: stem or continuative form of おす (osu) [godan]

Etymology 2

The 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 食す (osu, “to eat; to drink; to wear; to govern”, honorific term).

Noun

おし • (oshi)

hawk feed

Verb

おし • (oshi)

stem or continuative form of 食す (osu) [godan]

Etymology 3

From Old Japanese.

Adjective

おし • (oshi)

(archaic) Classical Japanese form of 惜しい (oshii)

Etymology 4

From おうし (ōshi), historical ofushi. Attested in The Tale of Genji.

Noun

おし • (oshi)

muteness (the inability to speak)

a mute person

Etymology 5

From Old Japanese; attested in the Nihon Shoki (720). It is likely that the Ryukyuan languages borrowed the Japanese word due to the suffix *tori (“bird”) and the lack of an initial w-.

Noun

おし • (oshi)

(archaic) synonym of おしどり (“mandarin duck”)

a style of 家紋 (kamon, “family crest”), like the mandarin duck

Etymology 6

Interjection

おし • (oshi)

[from 1004] alert made in the imperial palace by a forerunner when an emperor passes by or a meal is served

[from 1014] ceremonial phrase when receiving a plaque from the emperor: I humbly take it

Etymology 7

Likely derived from the same source as 私 (watashi → atashi); cf. 私 (ashi).

Pronoun

おし • (oshi)

(dialect, Tochigi) first-person pronoun: I, me

Synonym: 私 (watashi, standard)

(informal, dialect, Gifu, Aichi, Hyōgo, Wakayama, Ehime, Nagasaki, etc.) second-person pronoun: you

Synonyms: お前 (omae), 君 (kimi)

おしなにしてんねや

oshi nani shiten ne ya

Yo, whatcha be doin? (Wakayama dialect)

Interjection

おし • (oshi)

(dialect, Hyōgo) used to call on a wife

Etymology 8

Metathesis of 塩 (shio).

Noun

おし • (oshi)

[from 1892–obsolete] (thief cant) salt

Synonym: 塩 (shio, standard)
