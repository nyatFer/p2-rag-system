# たる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

たる (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Verb

たる • (taru)

attributive form (連体形) of たり (tari, “to be”)

教育者たる私は学生たちを正しい道に導くのだ。

Kyōikusha taru watashi wa gakusei tachi o tadashii michi ni michibiku no da.

As an educator, I will lead students to the right track.

Usage notes

In Modern Japanese, chiefly used after a noun, usually an occupation, rank, position etc., indicating emphasis or responsibility, or with a certain class of adjective, see Category:Japanese たり-tari adjectives.

Etymology 2

Suffix

たる • (-taru) godan (stem たり (-tari), past たった (-tatta))

(verb suffix, Kansai) contraction of てやる (-te yaru)

Synonyms: (standard) てやる (-te yaru), (standard) てあげる (-te ageru)
