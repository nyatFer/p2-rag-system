# じゃ (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

じゃ (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Syllable

じゃ • (ja)

The hiragana syllable じゃ (ja). Its equivalent in katakana is ジャ (ja).

Etymology 2

Contraction of では (de wa).

Conjunction

じゃ • (ja)

(colloquial) so, then—used for connecting to a reasonable conclusion, supposition

Interjection

じゃ • (ja)

well; then; so

by extension from the "so then" meaning: bye, goodbye

じゃね！ ― Ja ne! ― See you!

Etymology 3

Contraction of である (de aru, “to be, to exist”).

Verb

じゃ • (ja)

to be

わしはわしじゃ。

Washi wa washi ja.

I am me.

Usage notes

Variation on the Japanese copula だ (da) used in western Japan, such as in Chūgoku, Shikoku, or Kyūshū; particularly common in Hiroshima. The use of じゃ (ja) is also common in fictional dialogue attributed to elderly characters. In Kansai, じゃ (ja) changed to や (ya) in the late Edo period.

Etymology 4

Kanji reading.

Noun

じゃ • (ja)

蛇: generic name for a large snake; a heavy drinker

邪: a moral wrong; a bad attitude; maliciousness, wickedness; such a person

麝: short for 麝香 (jakō, “musk”); short for 麝香鹿 (jakōjika, “musk deer”)
