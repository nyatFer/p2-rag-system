# っぱなし (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

っぱなし (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Intensification of 放し (hanashi, “releasing”).

Suffix

っぱなし • (-ppanashi)

mistakenly leaving something in a certain state

開けっぱなしのドア ― akeppanashi no doa ― door that was left open

(doing something) constantly, non-stop

電話が鳴りっぱなしだ。 ― Denwa ga narippanashi da. ― The phone's been ringing off the hook.

Usage notes

Attaches to a verb in ren'yōkei.
