# ございます (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

ございます (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Sound change from 御座ります (gozarimasu, “to exist”, polite form).

Verb

ございます • (gozaimasu)

(formal) polite form of ある (aru): to exist

Synonym: あります (arimasu)

おあつらえ向きのお品がございます。

O-atsuraemuki no o-shina ga gozaimasu.

We have items that are suitable for you.

(literally, “Items that are suitable for you exist.”)

(auxiliary, formal) polite form of ある (aru)

お早うございます。

O-hayō gozaimasu.

Good morning.

ありがとうございました。

Arigatō gozaimashita.

Thank you.

誠に嬉しゅうございます。

Makoto ni ureshū gozaimasu.

I am truly happy.

死にとうございます。

Shinitō gozaimasu.

I want to die.

Usage notes

Usage in polite speech varies between speakers. Primarily used only to indicate existence or location, as in:

食品売り場は地下にございます。

Shokuhin uriba wa chika ni gozaimasu.

The food department is in the basement.

かばん売り場は １階でございます。

Kaban uriba wa ikkai de gozaimasu.

The bag department is on the 1st floor.
