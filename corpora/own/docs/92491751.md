# ず (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

ず (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

The hiragana character す (su) with a dakuten (゛).

Syllable

ず • (zu)

The hiragana syllable ず (zu). Its equivalent in katakana is ズ (zu).

Etymology 2

From Old Japanese. Generally considered to be a combination of negation suffix ぬ (-nu), in its 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “continuative or stem form”) of に (ni), + verb す (su, “to do”).

/nisu/ → */nzu/ → /zu/

Appears both as zu and as the older nisu form in the Man'yōshū poetry anthology.

Suffix

ず • (-zu)

not doing

何も知らず

nani mo shirazu

not knowing anything

Usage notes

Just like the older negation suffix ぬ (-nu) and the modern negation suffix ない (-nai), ず (-zu) comes after the 未然形 (mizenkei, “irrealis or incomplete form”) of a verb.

The suffix ず (-zu) is more formal than the everyday negation suffix ない (-nai).

The kanji spelling 不 attaches to verbs as a prefix in kanbun style, forming jukujikun readings such as 不知 (shirazu) or 不悪 (ashikarazu).
