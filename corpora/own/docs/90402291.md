# に於いて (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

に於いて (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology

に (ni, “at”) + おいて (oite), the conjunctive -te form of 置く (oku, “put, place”). Literally '(having been) placed or put at'. Originally from kanbun kundoku.

Particle

に於いて • (ni oite)

in, at, on

Usage notes

Usually spelled in hiragana, as において.
