# 及び (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

及び (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From the 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 及ぶ (oyobu, “to reach”).

Compare Middle Korean 및 (mìch) / Korean 및 (mit) from Middle Korean 및다〮 (mìchtá).

Conjunction

及び • (oyobi)

and, as well as

Synonyms: 並びに (narabi ni), また (mata), 且つ (katsu), と (to)

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in kana, as および (oyobi).

Used in conjunction with 並びに (narabi ni) to form complex lists, particularly in legal contexts. Narabi ni divides subsets within the list, while oyobi is used to conjoin more closely related items.

Noun
