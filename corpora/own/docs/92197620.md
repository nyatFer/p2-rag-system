# ちゃう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

ちゃう (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

/-te ɕimau/ → /-t͡ɕimau/ → /-t͡ɕau/

Contraction of ちまう (-chimau), itself a contraction of て (-te) +‎ しまう (shimau).

Suffix

ちゃう • (-chau)

(colloquial, chiefly Tōkyō) contraction of 〜てしまう

Usage notes

Attaches exactly like て (-te), including voicing to じゃう (jau) when て (-te) would voice to で (-de).

書く (kaku) → 書いて (kaite) → 書いてしまう (kaite shimau) → 書いちゃう (kaichau)

待つ (matsu) → 待って (matte) → 待ってしまう (matte shimau) → 待っちゃう (matchau)

呼ぶ (yobu) → 呼んで (yonde) → 呼んでしまう (yonde shimau) → 呼んじゃう (yonjau)

Etymology 2

/t͡ɕiɡau/ → /t͡ɕau/

Contraction of 違う (chigau).

Verb

ちゃう • (chau) intransitive
(colloquial, Kansai)

synonym of 違う (chigau, “to be different”)

ほかのとはちゃいます。 ― Hoka no to wa chaimasu. ― It's different from the others.

synonym of じゃない (ja nai, “not be”, negative copula)

笑てる場合ちゃうで！ ― Waroteru baai chau de! ― This is no laughing matter!

Usage notes

Limited to 終止形 (shūshikei, “predicative form”) and 連体形 (rentaikei, “attributive form”); other forms remain uncontracted.

May take ます (-masu) to form ちゃいます (chaimasu).

Trivia

Used in a famous Kansai dialect pun on チャウチャウ (chauchau, “Chow Chow (dog)”), ちゃうちゃう (chauchau, “no, no”), ちゃう (chau, “isn’t it?”), and ちゃう (chau, “no (it isn’t)”). One variant is:

チャウチャウちゃう？

Chauchau chau?

Isn’t that a chow chow?

ちゃうちゃう、チャウチャウちゃう。

Chauchau, chauchau chau.

No no, it’s not a chow chow.
