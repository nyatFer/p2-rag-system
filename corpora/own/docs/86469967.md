# 並びに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

並びに (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Conjunction

並びに • (narabi ni)

and, and also; used in conjunction with 及び (oyobi) to form complex lists, particularly in legal contexts.

Synonyms: 及び (oyobi), また (mata), 且つ (katsu), と (to)

Usage notes

並びに (narabi ni) divides subsets within the list, while 及び (oyobi) is used to conjoin more closely related items.
