# げ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

げ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

The hiragana character け (ke) with a dakuten (゛).

Syllable

げ • (ge)

The hiragana syllable げ (ge). Its equivalent in katakana is ゲ (ge).

Etymology 2

From 気 (ke, “spirit; mood; air”). Attested in the Kokin Wakashū.

Suffix

げ • (-ge)

[from early 900s] (unproductive) -esque; -ly; -ish; kind of; looks like... (attached to the stem form of an adjective, the continuative of verbs, and nouns)

嬉しげ、有り気、大人気

ureshige, arige, otonage

feeling happy, the way of being, being adulty

Synonyms: そう (-sō), らしい (-rashii) (both more common)

[from the early 1000s] (obsolete) a nominalizer suffixed by さ (-sa)

Usage notes

(senses 1 and 2): Used with psychological traits such as feelings; usage with physical traits such as color is rare.

When attaching to 良い (yoi) or 無い (nai), also appears with an intermediate さ (-sa-), producing よさげ (yosage) and なさげ (nasage). Compare the same behaviour of そう (sō).
