# こける (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

こける (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Verb

こける or コケる • (kokeru) ichidan (stem こけ (koke), past こけた (koketa))

to fall over

(of a performance) to flop

Etymology 2

Verb

こける • (kokeru) ichidan (stem こけ (koke), past こけた (koketa))

to become thin, hollow, haggard, skinny

Synonym: 痩せる (yaseru)

頰が痩けてしまった。

Hō ga kokete shimatta.

My cheeks have become lean.

Etymology 3

Suffix

こける • (-kokeru) ichidan (stem こけ (-koke), past こけた (-koketa))

(attached to the 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of a verb) to continue doing something unstoppably

笑いこける

waraikokeru

to laugh uncontrollably
