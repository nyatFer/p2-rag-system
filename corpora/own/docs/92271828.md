# 然らば (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

然らば (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Originally the hypothetical -ba conjugation of classical Japanese verb 然り (sari), itself ultimately a compound of 然 (sa, “like so, in such a manner”) + 有り (ari, “to be”, the classical form of modern 有る (aru)). Literally, “if that's the case; if it's like that; if that's the way it is”.

Interjection

然らば or 然らば • (saraba)

(archaic, now poetic) farewell, goodbye

Usage notes

This term is often spelled in hiragana, as さらば (saraba).

Sometimes prefixed with 御 (o-).
