# か (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

か (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 加 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

か • (ka)

The hiragana syllable か (ka). Its equivalent in katakana is カ (ka). It is the sixth syllable in the gojūon order; its position is か行あ段 (ka-gyō a-dan, “row ka, section a”).

Etymology 2

From Proto-Japonic *ka. False cognate of Malay and Indonesian -kah (“interrogative suffix”).

Particle

か • (ka)

(final)

(interrogative) marks a statement as a question

お元気ですか。 ― O-genki desu ka. ― How are you?

(with verbs in volitional) used to make suggestions: shall we … ?; could we … ?

行こうか。 ― Ikō ka. ― Shall we go?

(with ない (-nai)) used to issue commands: would you … !; will you … !

やめないか！ ― Yamenai ka! ― Will you stop!

(adverbial)

marks indefiniteness: some

誰か ― dare ka ― someone (literally, “who indefinite”)

何か ― nani ka ― something (literally, “what indefinite”)

誰かが何かを食べる。

Dare ka ga nani ka o taberu.

Someone eats something.

marks doubt or supposition: perhaps, maybe, whether ... or not

かも知れない ― ka mo shirenai ― perhaps (literally, “can't know whether”)

行くか知らない。 ― Iku ka shiranai. ― I don't know if he'll go.

どうすればいいかわからない。 ― Dō sureba ii ka wakaranai. ― I don't know what I should do.

行くかと思いますが…… ― Iku ka to omoimasu ga…… ― I think he'll go (but I'm not sure)....

カ, ヵ: used to mark uncertainty in non-conversation factual statements, such as data or proofreading

時代：江戸中期ヵ ― Jidai: Edo-chūki ka ― Period: mid-Edo period?

壹升〔斗ヵ〕 ― ichimasu [-to ka] ― one masu (sic: to?)

(parallel-marking)

marks a disjunction: or

これか、それか、どっちか選んでよ。

Kore ka, sore ka, dotchika erande yo.

This or that, choose one.

Etymology 3

First cited to The Tale of Genji (c. 1001-1014).

Prefix

か • (ka-)

(emphatic, before an adjective, unproductive) very

‎か + ‎弱い (yowai) → ‎か弱い (kayowai)

‎か + ‎細い (hosoi) → ‎か細い (kabosoi)

Etymology 4

From Old Japanese. Only persists in stems. No longer productive in Modern Japanese.

Suffix

か • (-ka)

forms 形容動詞 (keiyō dōshi, “-na adjectives”) from stems, indicating the state or feeling of an object

Usage notes

May attach directly to a root.

May be followed in turn by -i adjective-forming suffix しい (-shii).

More rarely, may be followed by -i adjective-forming suffix い (-i).

Often follows -na adjective-forming suffixes や (ya) or ら (ra); see also やか (-yaka) and らか (-raka)
