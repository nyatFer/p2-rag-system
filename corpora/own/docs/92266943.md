# その上 (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

その上 (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

その (sono, “(of) that”) + 上 (ue, “(on) top)”)

Conjunction

その上 • (sono ue)

in addition; on top of that

Etymology 2

その (sono, “(of) that”) + 上 (kami, “a time in the past”)

Noun

その上 • (sono kami)

that time (in the past)
