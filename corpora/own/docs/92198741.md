# 加之 (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

加之 (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 然 (shika, “so”) + のみ (nomi, “only”) + ならず (narazu, the classical negative form of なり (nari, “to be”)), being equivalent to そうだけではなく, and thus possessing the meaning ‘it is not only so…’.

Conjunction

加之 • (shika nomi narazu)

in addition; moreover; further
