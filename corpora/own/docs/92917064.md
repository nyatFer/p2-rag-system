# 如 (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

如 (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Readings

Go-on: にょ (nyo, Jōyō)

Kan-on: じょ (jo, Jōyō)

Kun: ごとし (gotoshi, 如し)、しく (shiku, 如く)、ゆく (yuku, 如く)、もし (moshi, 如し)、もころ (mokoro)

Nanori: ゆ (yu)、ゆき (yuki)、ね (ne)、よし (yoshi)

Etymology 1

From Middle Chinese 如 (MC nyo, “as if, as though”).

Noun

如 • (nyo)

(Buddhism) tathata, thusness, suchness, eternal truth

Etymology 2

From Middle Chinese 如 (MC nyo, “as if, as though”).

Affix

如 • (jo)

as if, likeness

Etymology 3

Stem of Classical Japanese adjective 如し (gotoshi, “like”).

Particle

如 • (goto)

(Classical Japanese, archaic, literary, poetic) like, similar to
