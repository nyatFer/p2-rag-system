# さ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

さ (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Derived in the Heian period from writing the man'yōgana kanji 左 in the cursive sōsho style.

Syllable

さ • (sa)

The hiragana syllable さ (sa). Its equivalent in katakana is サ (sa). It is the eleventh syllable in the gojūon order; its position is さ行あ段 (sa-gyō a-dan, “row sa, section a”).

Etymology 2

Cognate with し (shi).

Pronoun

さ • (sa)

third person personal pronoun; they

Etymology 3

From Old Japanese.

Suffix

さ • (-sa)

-ness

綺麗 → 綺麗さ

kirei → kireisa

beautiful → beauty (な-adjective)

悲しい → 悲しさ

kanashii → kanashisa

sad → sadness (い-adjective)

Usage notes

Used with na-adjectives and i-adjectives.

Has a different connotation from み (-mi). See that entry for explanation.

Etymology 4

Particle

さ • (sa)

(dialectal Kanto, Tōhoku, Hokkaidō) sentence-ending particle indicating emphasis

(colloquial, men's speech) sentence-ending particle indicating familiarity or assertion

大丈夫さ。気にしないでくれ。

Daijōbu sa. Ki ni shinai de kure.

It's fine, don't worry about it.

(informal) filler particle similar to ね (ne)

それでさ、凄く怒られたんだよ。

Sore de sa, sugoku okorareta n da yo.

Then, I really got yelled at.

Usage notes

As a male sentence-ending particle, less strong than ぞ (zo) or ぜ (ze).

Etymology 6

Adverb

さ • (sa)

(Classical Japanese) that way, like that

Synonym: そう (sō)

Usage notes

Nowadays mostly in fixed expressions or compounds.
