# だす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

だす (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Verb

Etymology 2

Likely from で +‎ やす. See further etymology at です.

Verb

だす • (dasu)

(dialectal, Ōsaka, archaic or obsolete elsewhere) synonym of です (desu)

Usage notes

Traditional polite form of the auxiliary verb だ; compare です, である. Largely obsolete since the Meiji period, but still used in dialects of the Osaka area.

Etymology 3

Shift from だつ (datsu).

Noun

だす • (dasu)

synonym of 駄津 (datsu, “needlefish”)
