# 草臥れる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

草臥れる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Verb

草臥れる • (kutabireru) intransitive ichidan (stem 草臥れ (kutabire), past 草臥れた (kutabireta))

to be exhausted, to be very tired

to be or appear old, used, or worn

くたびれた紙 ― kutabireta kami ― worn paper

(auxiliary, attaches to the ren'yōkei of another verb) to be tired of, to have enough of (chiefly in 待ちくたびれる (machikutabireru))
