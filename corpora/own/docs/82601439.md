# やがる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

やがる (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

/-i aɡaru//-e aɡaru/ → /-jaɡaru/

Prothesis of 上がる (agaru), caused by almost all Japanese verbs having a 連用形 (ren'yōkei) ending in -i or -e.

Verb

やがる • (yagaru) godan (stem やがり (yagari), past やがった (yagatta))

Shows contempt for the action described.

勝手なことを言いやがって

katte na koto o iiyagatte

throwing words around carelessly on one's own

失せやがれ

useyagare

get the fuck out

Usage notes

Attaches to the 連用形 (ren'yōkei) of a verb.
