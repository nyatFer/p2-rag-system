Etymology 2

From the 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 宣る (noru). Cognate with Japanese 典 (nori).

Also said to be from the 連用形 (ren'yōkei, “stem or continuative form”) of the verb 乗る (noru).

Noun

のり • (nori)

rule, regulation, law

Synonyms: 決まり (kimari), 規則 (kisoku), 法規 (hōki), 法律 (hōritsu)
