Etymology

Mimicing erhua ("Chinese 兒 / 儿") in northern Chinese dialects. Or, from kyōwa-go (協和語).

Particle

アル • (aru)

(foreignism, fiction) Stereotypical sentence-final particle used by Chinese.

(offensive, ethnic slur) Mimicking Chinese speech; ching chong
