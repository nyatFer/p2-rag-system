# 癖に (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

癖に (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Conjunction

癖に • (kuse ni)

and yet; though; when; despite being; in spite of being

新人のくせに。

Shinjin no kuse ni.

You're just a greenhorn!

黙れ！何もできないくせに。

Damare! Nani mo dekinai kuse ni.

Shut up! You don't even know how to do anything.
