# ならば (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

ならば (Japanese, particle, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Classical hypothetical form of copula なり (nari, “to be”), formed by attaching ば (ba) to the irrealis stem なら (nara).

Particle

ならば • (naraba)

alternative form of なら (nara, “if”, conditional particle)

Etymology 2

Classical hypothetical form of 成る (naru, “to become; to come into being”), formed by attaching ば (ba) to the irrealis stem なら (nara).

As a conjunction, first cited to the late 1500s.

Particle

ならば • (naraba)

[from late 1500s] alternative form of なら (nara, “if”, conditional particle)
