# っこ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

っこ (Japanese, suffix, Wiktionary)

Etymology 1

Variant of the suffix こ (ko, “by two people, mutually”), possibly from nominalizing suffix こと (koto).

Suffix

っこ • (-kko)

mutually, reciprocally, to one another

投げっこする

nagekko suru

to throw to one another; to play catch

宿題の写しっこ

shukudai no utsushikko

copying one another's homework

alternative form of っこない: in no way, certainly not

Usage notes

Suffixed to the 連用形 (ren'yōkei) continuative or conjunctive form of a verb.

Etymology 2

Originally a slightly jocular variant of 子 (ko, “child”).

Suffix

っこ • (-kko)

っ子: child of, typical resident of

江戸っ子 ― Edokko ― true Tokyoite, child of Edo

Usage notes

Suffixed to a proper noun or a noun indicating a place
