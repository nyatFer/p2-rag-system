# 腐る (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

腐る (Japanese, auxiliary verb, Wiktionary)

Etymology

Related to 糞 (kuso) and 臭い (kusai).

Verb

腐る • (kusaru) intransitive godan (stem 腐り (kusari), past 腐った (kusatta))

to rot; to decompose

死体が腐る。 ― Shitai ga kusaru. ― A corpse rots.

to spoil; to expire; to go bad; go off

りんごが腐る。 ― Ringo ga kusaru. ― An apple spoils.

to corrode

to become corrupt; to degenerate

to be dejected

(figurative, business, of inventory) to not move

在庫が腐る。 ― Zaiko ga kusaru. ― Inventory doesn't move.

(derogatory, auxiliary with verb in ren'yōkei) used to denigrate the action of another

Synonym: やがる (yagaru)
