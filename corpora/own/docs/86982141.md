# 誰も (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

誰も (Japanese, conjunction, Wiktionary)

Etymology

From 誰 (dare, “who”) + も (mo, “even, also, too”, inclusive particle).

Pronoun

誰も • (daremo or dare mo)

(with negative verbs) no one, nobody

そんなことは誰も気にしない。

Sonna koto wa dare mo ki ni shinai.

No one cares about that.

anyone, anybody

誰もが何れ高齢者だ。

Daremo ga izure kōreisha da.

Everyone gets old eventually.

Usage notes

Unless followed by another particle, the term 誰も (dare mo) is used in negative constructions. For use in positive constructions, an additional particle is needed, as shown in the usage examples above.
